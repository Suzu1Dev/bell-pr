This policy explains how you receive a BELL PR Pro License Key, how to get a refund, and what happens to a refunded key. Capitalized terms have the meaning that the license terms in LICENSE.md (the "EULA") give them.

Delivery of your License Key

We deliver License Keys [OWNER: delivery method, either "instantly after payment, from a pool of pre-signed keys" or "by hand, by e-mail"]. If we deliver by hand, you receive your key by e-mail within [OWNER: N] business days after your payment.

A License Key is text that you paste into the License section of Settings, or pass to bell-pr license activate. Activating it needs no internet connection and sends nothing anywhere. A key from a pool of pre-signed keys contains no name or e-mail address. The License section of Settings and bell-pr license status show the dates your key carries: "updates until", the last release date of the Builds it covers, or the expiry date of a subscription.

Immediate delivery and statutory cancellation rights

A License Key is digital content that is not supplied on a physical medium. At checkout we ask you to agree that we deliver it immediately. Because of that consent:

  • Consumers in the EU and the EEA: you agree that we start supplying the key before the 14-day withdrawal period ends, and you acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once we have supplied it.
  • Consumers in Taiwan: the 7-day cancellation right for distance sales does not apply to digital content that is not supplied on a physical medium once you have agreed to its supply.

Our voluntary refund window below applies regardless of these rules. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under mandatory consumer law, for example when a product is faulty.

Refunds

You can ask for a full refund within [OWNER: 14 or 30] days of your purchase, for any reason. For an order with several seats, you can ask for a refund of the whole order or of some of its seats. The refund goes back to your original payment method through the Merchant of Record.

The free 14-day Pro trial in the app lets you try Pro before you buy. Please use it to check that Pro works for you.

After the refund window ends, we refund only where the law requires it.

What happens to a refunded key

When we refund an order or seats, or a payment is charged back, we revoke the License Keys for the refunded seats, and the Pro License for them ends (EULA section 16). A revocation covers a whole License Key, so if a key also holds seats that you keep, we send you a new key for those seats. Stop using Pro with those keys and remove them: in the License section of Settings, with bell-pr license deactivate, or by removing BELLPR_LICENSE_KEY if you set the key there.

Revoking a refunded key takes effect in builds released after the revocation; older builds keep accepting it offline. Using a refunded key in any build breaches the EULA.
