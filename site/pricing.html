Pricing

Free covers the whole contribution loop, one contribution at a time. Pro adds bigger and parallel agent teams, GitHub-wide discovery and coaching on maintainer feedback. Both keep you in charge: nothing is published until you approve it.

Free

No account, no license key and no time limit.

For learning a codebase while you make your first contributions.

  • The whole loop: analysis, plan approval, agent team, review, explanations and Draft PR
  • One contribution at a time; other runs wait in the queue
  • A Lead, two teammates, a Reviewer and a Test analyst
  • Learning notes for every contribution
  • The sample project, with Pro features shown on sample data

Licensing questions

How is a Pro license checked?

On your machine, offline. BELL PR verifies the license key against a public key built into the app. There is no license server and no account.

What happens when the trial ends?

BELL PR returns to Free. Everything you already have stays readable: runs, explanations, notes, recommendations and discovery results.

Do I need an AI subscription?

You need the Codex CLI or Claude Code, installed and signed in. BELL PR uses that CLI with your own account, for example your Claude plan or Anthropic API key with Claude Code, and usage counts against that account. A Pro license does not include AI usage.

Paste the key into the License section of Settings, or run bell-pr license activate. Activation works offline.

What does a license key contain?

A license ID, the plan, the number of seats and its dates. It holds a name and an e-mail address only if they were added when the key was issued. The key stays on your computer; the privacy policy has the details.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes. A seat belongs to one person, who may use it on all of their own computers. A team buys one seat per person.

What happens when the updates period ends?

Pro stays on in every build released up to the "updates until" date that your key shows in Settings. Builds released later run as Free unless you renew, and everything you made stays readable.
