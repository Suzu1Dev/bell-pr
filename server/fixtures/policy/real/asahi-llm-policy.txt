Due to these legal and ethical issues, we broadly forbid the use of generative AI tooling for material contributions to Asahi Linux. Enforcement may vary depending on the seriousness of the infraction. A GitHub issue drafted by an LLM may simply be closed with reference to this policy. A contributor using an LLM to interpret a trace made using the m1n1 hypervisor may be issued with a first and final warning. A contributor found to have concealed extensive LLM use will be banned immediately, particularly if they may have accessed unreleased Apple material.
